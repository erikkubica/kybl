Overview

One window for every place your files live.

Split it into panes, give each its own view, and keep a terminal next to the files it works on.

Connect

Bring every bucket, drive and server.

S3 and anything S3-compatible, Azure, Google Cloud Storage, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, WebDAV, SFTP, FTP, SSH and this computer.

Browse and preview

Find it, look at it, open it.

Thumbnails, Quick Look, a full info panel, search through every subfolder and a command palette for the rest.

Work with files

All the file manager moves, on every connection.

Menus grouped the way you think: open, edit, send, make, about, keep and delete.

Move data

Copy, mirror and sync between any two places.

Transfers you can follow, jobs that survive a restart, and folders that stay in step on their own.

Workspaces

A layout for every job.

Each workspace tab keeps its panes, places and terminals. Save one as a recipe and start it fresh any time.

Settings

Make it yours.

Themes and colour schemes, fonts and density, transfer limits, custom actions, drives and every connection in one list.

Phone

The same connections in your pocket.

Android and iPhone, with a touch layout, photo backup and transfers that keep going in the background.

Ready when you are.

Free for macOS, Windows, Linux, Android and iPhone.
